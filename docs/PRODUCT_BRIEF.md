# Morrow product brief

Morrow is a fictional premium athletic-lifestyle shoe brand for people who move through varied, ordinary days. The problem is not a lack of shoe choice; it is unclear claims, confusing fit information, and stressful buying journeys. Morrow promises “useful optimism”: clear product language, calm purchasing, and help close to every decision. Its differentiator is evidence-minded merchandising with a warm, energetic identity. The sample range covers running, walking, training, court, trail, hiking, recovery, sandals, boots, and lifestyle at entry, middle, and premium demo price points. The assumed launch market is the United States, in English and USD. The prototype objective is to validate discovery-to-support journeys before committing to vendors or legal policy.

Audience priorities: new customers first; then returning guests, gift buyers, parents, and support-seeking customers. Context includes mobile-first shoppers, older users, keyboard and screen-reader users, low-bandwidth situations, and future multilingual markets.

Brand promise: Shoes for the miles that count—and all the good stuff between them.

Manifesto: Tomorrow is not a finish line. It is the next useful step. We make room for movement without making noise about it: clear choices, considered details, and shoes ready for real days.

Price architecture (sample): entry $68–$89; core $112–$148; premium $158–$189. Product names use optimistic time, place, or movement ideas; technologies use plain functional language; color names stay concrete; collaborations and environmental claims require approval.
